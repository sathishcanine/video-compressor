Privacy Policy

VidPress (application ID com.vidcompressor.vidcompressor) — Effective date: May 15, 2026

This policy describes how VidPress (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application. It reflects the behavior of the App as built from the project’s main branch at the time this page was published.

Summary

VidPress is a video compression tool. Compression runs on your device. We do not operate a login or user accounts in the App, and we do not intentionally collect personal data on our own servers for core App features. Third-party services (notably Google advertising) may collect or process data under their own policies, as described below.

Information the App accesses on your device

  • Videos and media you choose. You pick videos from your photo library or storage so the App can compress them. Processing uses libraries on the device (including FFmpeg-based compression).
  • Saving and sharing. If you save a compressed file or use share features, that happens through your device’s photo library, files, or the system share sheet you control.

Local processing

Video compression and related operations are performed locally on your device. The App is not designed to upload your video files to servers we operate for compression.

Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show ads (for example, rewarded and native formats). Google may use identifiers and related data to personalize or measure ads. Their practices are governed by Google’s policies, for example:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob and data

Where available on your device, you can limit ad tracking or reset advertising identifiers through your platform settings (e.g., Android advertising settings, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency and privacy settings).

Network access

The App may use the network for advertising and related services, and for dependencies that fetch resources over the internet when applicable (for example, fonts or SDK components). Routine use of those features is subject to the respective providers’ terms and privacy notices.

Permissions

Depending on platform and version, the App may request permissions such as access to photos or video media (and on some Android versions, broader storage read access) so you can select and save videos. The system permission prompts explain the purpose at install or runtime.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate steps.
